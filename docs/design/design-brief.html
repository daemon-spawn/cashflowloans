Gate 1

Strategy

The primary thing we want a visitor to do is start a loan application with their documents attached. Everything else supports that or removes a reason not to do it.

Gate 1 · Blockers

Open questions for the owner

The manual says never to invent business facts. These answers go straight into headlines, proof and FAQ copy.

Pricing needs a compliance check before it goes on the homepage The site advertises a flat 50% fee for one month, plus another 50% on the balance for each missed due date. The National Credit Act caps interest and fees on short-term credit, and 50% a month is far above those caps unless the product is structured differently, for example as a pawn transaction. Please have the owner confirm with their NCR compliance advisor exactly how the cost must be described and disclosed. The calculator and hero will show whatever they confirm.
  • NCR registration number (NCRCP…) and the registered business address, both shown in the footer.
  • Testimonials: name or initials, town, loan type, and consent to publish.
  • Do you lend to people who are blacklisted or under debt review? This is a common question, and the answer has to be truthful.
  • Which collateral is accepted (phones, laptops, vehicles, property), and is there a maximum amount per type? Is R100,000 also the unsecured maximum?
  • How quickly does a secured loan pay out after collection?
  • Any real photos: team, office, courier collections, storage? Real imagery beats stock for trust.
  • Proof we can state honestly: years trading, number of loans paid out, Google rating.
  • Blog: migrate the 8 articles as they are, or rewrite them?
  • Analytics: Vercel Analytics, Plausible or GA4?

Gate 5 · Interaction

Motion rules

Lenis and GSAP stay, used lightly. Everything must work with motion turned off.

  • Lenis smooths scrolling only. No scroll hijacking, no pinned sequences that hold the visitor back.
  • One orchestrated moment: the four-step process diagram draws itself as it enters the screen.
  • Calculator numbers count smoothly to their new value as the slider moves. This is feedback, so it stays.
  • Sections fade up once as they enter the screen, from a visible resting state. Nothing waits at opacity 0.
  • Hover and focus states on every control. Visible focus ring in marigold.
  • prefers-reduced-motion turns all of the above off.
